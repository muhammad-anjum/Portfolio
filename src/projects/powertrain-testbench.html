Build a platform for integrated powertrain testing.

The testbench was Aztec Electric Racing’s first powertrain test platform. It combined electrical, mechanical and hydraulic components so the team could wire, calibrate, troubleshoot and iterate before full-vehicle integration. It was built and iterated with cooling-loop and wiring-harness integration.

Design, wire, calibrate, test.

I worked as Powertrain and Ergonomics Engineer from August 2022 to May 2023, on both the testbench and the driver pedal controls.

Responsibilities

  • Designed and fabricated the functional testbench, with electrical, mechanical and hydraulic components.
  • Programmed, calibrated and tuned electronic components through the motor inverter.
  • Used design matrices and physical analyses to select commercial and custom parts.
  • Traced and connected wiring to the vehicle harness, and applied circuit principles to troubleshoot wiring and circuitry.
  • Modeled preliminary driver foot-pedal designs in SolidWorks and integrated the electrical components with the pedal box.
  • Programmed pedal travel for the desired motor response and driver comfort.
  • Documented the work for reference and validation.
