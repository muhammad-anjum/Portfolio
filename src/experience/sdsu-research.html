From battery systems to battery materials.

My battery work so far has been at the vehicle level. This research adds the materials and the laboratory.

I work in Dr. Lingping Kong’s Electrochemical Energy Lab at San Diego State University, part of SDSU’s advanced energy-storage research environment. Within the group I own the anode recycling and characterization project.

The work expands my experience from vehicle-level battery systems, such as the Stag’s battery pack and the Aztec Electric Racing accumulator, into battery materials and laboratory research.

Research group website. The group’s other projects and publications are described there; this page covers only my own project.
