01 · Brief

Make the electrical conversion work as one vehicle.

The assignment spans low-voltage distribution, controls, instrumentation, high-voltage energy storage, and commissioning. The work begins with component selection and ends with a tested, integrated system.

Hands-on across the stack.

  • Built and tested high-voltage battery packs, including cell validation and BMS configuration.
  • Owned the electrical conversion end to end through final commissioning.
